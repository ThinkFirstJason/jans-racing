The Team

Calm under pressure.
Flat out on track.

Jan Qualkenbush started Jan's Towing in 1979, straight out of high school in Glendora, California. For over forty years the trucks have run 24/7 across the San Gabriel Valley, trusted by police departments in Azusa, Glendora and Covina.

Along the way Jan quietly backed driver after driver on West Coast short tracks — no spotlight, no fanfare. In 2024 that support became a team of its own: Jan's Racing, competing in the ARCA Menards Series West.

“Calm under pressure, professional to the core, kind even when the job was grueling.”

The standard Jan's was built on — in the tow truck and on pit road.

Lap By Lap

From tow hook
to checkered flag

  2. 1979

    Jan's Towing opens

    Jan Qualkenbush starts a 24/7 towing company in Glendora that will serve the San Gabriel Valley for over four decades.

  3. Decades

    The quiet sponsor

    Jan backs driver after driver up and down West Coast short tracks — for the love of racing, not the credit.

  4. 2024

    Jan's Racing hits the grid

    The team debuts in the ARCA Menards Series West, grabbing a pole and front-running finishes at tracks including Irwindale. Kyle Keller wins at Tri-City Raceway.

  5. 2025

    Daytona & a Rookie of the Year

    First ARCA start at Daytona. Robbie Kennealy wins from the pole at All American Speedway, scores 10 top-10s in 12 starts, finishes third in the championship and takes Rookie of the Year.

  6. 2026

    Two cars, two threats

    16-year-old Cole Denton joins in the No. 71 and wins in his third start at Tucson, then again at Tri-City, both from the pole. Kennealy takes the pole at Colorado and runs third in points in the No. 1.
